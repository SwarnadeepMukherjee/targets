    Deep focus only. Phone away. No multitasking. Timer running.

    Minimum 20 minutes of deliberate learning on a topic from Section 05.

    Sit in Boredom. Do Not Reach.
    When idle with nothing to do — tolerate it. The reflex to grab your phone is the enemy.
03

Weekly Non-Negotiable

Once per week, conduct a ruthless self-audit. No kindness. No excuses. No skipping.

Review: What rules did you break? What excuses did you make? What habits slipped? Where did your confidence waver? What needs to change this week?

Write it down. Fix it. Move forward. The review is not optional — it is a non-negotiable listed rule. Missing it triggers a punishment.
